About the project

Travelist (code name Wayfarer) is a cross-platform travel companion for iOS, Android and the web, built with Expo and Supabase. It covers 198 cities in 52 countries and 22,756 attractions.

Before you go, it builds a checklist personalised to your passports (visa, passport validity, power, weather, money, safety, practical info). To explore, it offers a map of attractions with city pages: Wikipedia stories, famous people, community walk lists and reviews. To walk, it optimises a route between the places you pick, saves it as a trip, shares it and opens it in Google or Apple Maps.

Goal

Put everything a traveller needs in one app: what to sort out before leaving, what is worth seeing, and the best order to see it on foot.

What it does

Before you go

A checklist personalised to your passports, using weather, exchange-rate and visa data served through Supabase Edge Functions.

Explore

A MapLibre map of attractions with city pages, community walk lists and reviews, in English and Portuguese.

Walk

Optimised walking routes between the places you pick, saved as trips, shared with other travellers and opened in Google or Apple Maps.

A real data pipeline

A Python pipeline combines Wikidata, OpenStreetMap, Wikipedia and Commons into committed SQL seeds, so the reference data is reproducible.
