CV Fit AI

AI-powered web application that tailors your resume to a specific job.

Visit cvfitai.com ↗View on GitHub ↗
JavaSpring BootOpenAIStripe

About the project

CV Fit AI (the codebase is called Resume Tailor) lets users upload a resume in PDF or DOCX format, paste a job description, and receive a rewritten version optimised for ATS, with job-specific keywords, explanations of the changes, and final files to download as PDF and DOCX.

The application extracts the text from the original document, validates the job description, deducts one credit and sends the context to the OpenAI API. If processing fails the credit is refunded automatically, and each result is saved to the user's history.

Goal

Cut the time it takes to customise a resume for each application, and ship it as a complete SaaS-style product: authentication, history, a credit system, Stripe checkout, email notifications and Docker-based deployment.

What it does

AI resume rewriting

OpenAI GPT-4o rewrites the resume around the target role and its keywords. The model is configurable.

PDF and DOCX pipeline

Reads uploads with Apache PDFBox and POI, then generates the final PDF (iText 8) and DOCX files.

Credits and payments

One credit per generation, one free credit on sign-up, credit packs through Stripe Checkout and idempotent webhook processing.

Safe by default

BCrypt passwords, CSRF protection, single-use reset tokens, signed Stripe webhooks, sanitised job descriptions and word limits.
