About me

The engineer behind the code.

I'm a software engineer who builds complete products from the database to the screen. My backends are Java and Spring Boot or C# and ASP.NET Core, with TypeScript clients for the web, mobile and desktop, and Python where the data or the models need it.

IERailMetrics turns the Irish Rail realtime feed into live boards and delay analytics. CV Fit AI rewrites resumes with OpenAI behind a credit system and Stripe. TechRat gamifies studying for tech jobs across web, mobile and desktop. Travelist plans trips across 198 cities, and OctoSplitter separates songs into stems on your own GPU.

I care about the parts that make software dependable: versioned schema migrations, bounded caches and retention, automatic refunds when a job fails, CI that deploys with a rollback, and an architecture that stays easy to change.
